Safe Route Planning and Route Justification for Mission-Critical Systems

Team ALPAR (Agent-Based Limit-Aware Planning and Adaptive Routing) · CMPE 491 Senior Design Project I · Fall 2026-2027

Project description

Mission-critical systems such as unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) and unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) operate in uncertain and constantly changing field conditions. Classic route planning algorithms compute a route once under static assumptions, so they fall short when a new obstacle appears, communication is lost, or the energy budget shrinks.

This project develops an agentic AI based decision and planning architecture that replans safely and instantly when conditions change. It also reports why the chosen route was preferred over its alternatives, using a language model, so that operators can trust the system's decisions. The expected contributions are real-time replanning in crisis scenarios (risk exposure, no-go zone violations), explainable AI for route choice, and dynamic weighting based on a safe energy reserve and uninterrupted communication quality.
