Safe route planning and route justification for mission-critical systems

Agent-Based Limit-Aware Planning and Adaptive Routing. An agentic AI planner for UAVs and UGVs that replans when conditions change and explains why it chose the route it did.

CMPE 491 Senior Design Project I, Fall 2026–2027, TED University.

About the project

Project description

This project aims to develop an intelligent and safe route planning system for mission-critical autonomous and nonautonomous vehicles, including UAVs, UGVs, and search-and-rescue robots.

Unlike conventional navigation systems, the proposed Agentic AI-based architecture dynamically evaluates environmental risks, mission priorities, and operational constraints. By combining adaptive route planning with deterministic safety verification, the system aims to respond to unexpected threats through real-time replanning while ensuring compliance with predefined safety constraints.

Real-time replanning
Safe replanning in crisis scenarios such as rising risk exposure or no-go zone violations.
Explainable route choice
A language model explains why the selected route was preferred over its alternatives.
Dynamic weighting
Route costs adapt to a safe energy reserve and uninterrupted communication quality.
